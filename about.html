01 Joinville, BrazilJoinville, Brasil

It started with a lanchonete.Tudo começou com uma lanchonete.

My dad and his father opened one of the first lanchonetes in Joinville. Foodservice has been in my family from the start.Meu pai e o pai dele abriram uma das primeiras lanchonetes de Joinville. O food service está na minha família desde o começo.

But my dad always had his eye on America and the American dream.Mas meu pai sempre teve os olhos nos Estados Unidos e no sonho americano.

02 The American dreamO sonho americano

At 19, he applied for a visa and moved to Chicago.Aos 19 anos, ele pediu o visto e se mudou para Chicago.

On his visa application, there was a question about why he wanted to go to Chicago. His answer:No pedido de visto, havia uma pergunta sobre por que ele queria ir para Chicago. A resposta dele:

"I want to go to the Taste of Chicago.""Eu quero ir ao Taste of Chicago."

A food festival. That was the dream. Remember it, because it comes back.Um festival de comida. Esse era o sonho. Guarde isso, porque ele volta.

05 Full circleO ciclo se fecha

Then I started my own food business, and it landed at the Taste of Chicago.Aí eu abri meu próprio negócio de comida, e ele foi parar no Taste of Chicago.

In 2023 I launched Frios Chicago, a mobile gourmet ice cream pop business with a truck and carts for indoor and outdoor pop-ups. It did more than $70,000 in revenue in its first five months, with placements in stores and cocktail bars and spots at Lakeview's Market Days and Andersonville's Midsommarfest.Em 2023 lancei a Frios Chicago, um negócio de picolés gourmet com caminhão e carrinhos para eventos internos e externos. Foram mais de US$ 70 mil em receita nos primeiros cinco meses, com produtos em lojas e bares de coquetéis e presença no Market Days de Lakeview e no Midsommarfest de Andersonville.

And in September 2023, Frios was a vendor at the Taste of Chicago in Grant Park, the same festival my dad wrote on his visa application, and we even ended up on the news.E em setembro de 2023, a Frios esteve no Taste of Chicago, no Grant Park, o mesmo festival que meu pai escreveu no pedido de visto. E ainda fomos parar no noticiário.
