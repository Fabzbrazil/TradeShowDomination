Common Questions

What you're probably wondering.

What format is the playbook delivered in?
It's a Notion-based playbook — 7 connected pages that work as both a guide (read top to bottom) and an operational tracker (fill in your specific data). After you submit the form, you'll get instant access via direct link. You can also duplicate it into your own Notion workspace and customize freely.
Do I need a Notion account?
You can view the playbook in any browser without an account. If you want to duplicate it into your own workspace and edit it, you'll need a free Notion account — which takes about 60 seconds to create.
Is this just for the National Restaurant Show, or other tradeshows too?
The frameworks (4-question filter, 20/45/35 budget rule, 48-hour follow-up, context over contacts) apply to any B2B tradeshow. The specific tactics and pricing examples are calibrated to the National Restaurant Show. If you exhibit at other foodservice shows, the playbook still works — you'll just need to adjust the specific tactic costs and infrastructure assumptions.
What if I'm a Brazilian company looking to enter the US market?
I work with Brazilian foodservice, beverage, equipment, and technology companies looking to break into the US market through the National Restaurant Show. I'm originally from Brazil, fluent in Portuguese, and understand both sides of the market entry challenge. Get the playbook first, then reach out and we'll talk specifically about your situation.
What happens after I download it?
You'll get instant access to the playbook. You'll also get occasional emails from me with foodservice industry insights and tradeshow strategy content (the same stuff I post on Substack). I might also follow up personally if your company profile is a fit for deeper conversation. No spam. No high-pressure sales.
